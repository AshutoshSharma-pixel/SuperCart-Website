FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Is there really no setup fee?

Correct. We come to your store, set everything up, train your staff, and give you 1 month free. You only pay subscription costs after you see the true business value.

What phones do my customers need?

Any Android or iOS smartphone with a camera. No app download is required to browse or scan — the customer shopping app runs directly as a progressive web app inside their browser.

Do I need any special hardware?

No. SuperCart runs entirely on standard smartphones. Your customers use their own phones to scan. Your guard uses a basic Android phone. You manage everything from your phone or laptop browser.

Which cities is SuperCart available in?

Currently we're setting up pilot stores in Jaipur. We'll expand to other Rajasthan cities and then pan-India based on customer demand.
