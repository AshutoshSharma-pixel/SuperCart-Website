How It Works

THREE STEPS TO CASHIERLESS

A modern shopping flow designed to eliminate friction for both shopkeepers and customers.

01
📲

Scan Store QR

Customer scans the QR code at the entrance of your store to instantly begin a secure shopping session on their smartphone.

02
🛒

Scan & Add Items

Customers walk around the store scanning item barcodes directly using their phone. Their virtual cart builds in real-time.

03
✅

Pay & Exit

Pay instantly using UPI/Cards via Razorpay. Generates an exit receipt QR that the gate guard quickly scans to verify. Done!

THREE APPS, ONE PLATFORM

EVERYTHING YOUR STORE NEEDS

SuperCart is a complete ecosystem — not just one app. Three purpose-built products that work together seamlessly.

  • →
    Customer App Customers scan product barcodes with their phone camera, add to cart, and pay digitally via UPI, card or wallet. No dedicated hardware needed.
  • →
    Guard App Dedicated app for your exit guard. Scans customer exit QR, instantly shows APPROVED or DENIED. Works on any Android phone.
  • →
    Store Portal Manage your entire store from any phone or laptop browser. Add products by scanning barcodes, manage stock, view transactions, set discounts — no billing machine needed. This is your USP: zero hardware required.
