2. The Service

SuperCart provides a B2B SaaS cashierless shopping platform for retail stores in India. The platform includes:

  • Store Portal: Web dashboard for store owners to manage inventory, pricing, analytics, and store settings.
  • Customer Mobile App: Android and iOS progressive web / native interface allowing shoppers to scan product barcodes and pay digitally.
  • Guard Verification App: Android and iOS app allowing store staff to scan exit receipts and verify customer carts.
  • Backend API & Infrastructure: Database syncing, security verification, and processing systems.

3. Accounts & Subscriptions

  • Registration: Store owners must register with accurate, up-to-date, and complete business information.
  • Subscription Plans: Billing is charged monthly based on the selected tier:
    • Starter: ₹999/month (up to 1,000 transactions/month, 100 products limit)
    • Growth: ₹3,500/month (up to 5,000 transactions/month, 500 products limit)
    • Enterprise: ₹8,000/month (unlimited transactions, unlimited products, custom integrations)
  • Renewal: Subscriptions auto-renew automatically unless cancelled via the Store Portal prior to the billing renewal date.
  • Account Security: You are solely responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials and credentials issued to your store's guards.

4. Payment Terms

All subscription payments are processed securely via our payment gateway partner, Razorpay. All prices are stated in Indian Rupees (INR) and are inclusive of applicable taxes unless specified otherwise. Failed payments may result in immediate suspension of API access and app functionality for your store. Subscription upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing applied to the current cycle.

5. Store Owner Responsibilities

Store owners using the B2B SaaS platform agree to:

  • Maintain accurate, up-to-date product barcodes and pricing in the Store Portal.
  • Ensure gate guard staff are properly trained to use the Guard App for exits.
  • Display store entry QR codes prominently at store entrances.
  • Comply with all local retail laws, GST declarations, and consumer protection codes.
  • Refrain from using the platform for any fraudulent or illegal sales operations.
